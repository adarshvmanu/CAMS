The Engineering Team Behind CAMS

Honoring the founding engineers whose computer vision research, mathematical formulations, and core modules established the Classroom Attention Monitoring System.

AVM

Adarsh Varghese Manu

Project Lead • Head Pose Estimation

Architected the real-time 3D Head Pose Estimation module. Pioneered the Perspective-n-Point (solvePnP) algorithm integration and Euler angle decomposition (Pitch, Yaw, Roll) using MediaPipe 3D facial meshes to continuously monitor student classroom gaze orientation.

Project Lead 3D solvePnP Euler Decomposition System Integration OpenCV
AS

Archa Sekhar

CV Engineer • Yawn Detection Lead

Researched and implemented the Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) fatigue detection module. Developed facial landmark extraction protocols for lips and mouth boundaries, enabling non-invasive identification of student tiredness, yawning frequency, and fatigue onset in educational environments.

Yawn Kinematics Mouth Aspect Ratio Facial Landmarks Fatigue Analysis MediaPipe
BA

B Anand

CV Engineer • Eye Closure & EAR Lead

Engineered the Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) drowsiness detection engine. Analyzed upper and lower eyelid coordinates to compute blink duration, eye closure velocities, and microsleep occurrences, establishing the core alertness metric used across CAMS.

Eye Aspect Ratio Eyelid Kinematics Microsleep Detection Web Telemetry Real-Time CV

Foundational Research Tribute

The V2.0 evolution of CAMS directly builds upon the core research foundations established by Adarsh, Archa, and Anand. Their commitment to GPU-free, lightweight computer vision ensures that intelligent classroom monitoring remains accessible on standard edge hardware without costly cloud infrastructure.
