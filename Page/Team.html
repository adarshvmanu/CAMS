The Founding Engineering Team

Honoring the foundational computer vision engineers whose mathematical formulations, kinematic models, and core algorithms established the Classroom Attention Monitoring System (CAMS).

AVM

Adarsh Varghese Manu

Project Lead • 3D Head-Pose Estimation

Architected the real-time 3D Head-Pose Estimation module. Pioneered the Perspective-n-Point (solvePnP) algorithmic integration and Euler angle decomposition (Pitch, Yaw, Roll) using MediaPipe 3D face mesh coordinates to continuously monitor student classroom gaze orientation.

Project Lead 3D solvePnP Euler Decomposition System Architecture OpenCV
AS

Archa Sekhar

CV Engineer • Yawn & Fatigue Kinematics Lead

Researched and implemented the Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) fatigue detection engine. Developed facial landmark extraction protocols for lips and oral contours, enabling non-invasive identification of student tiredness, yawning frequency, and fatigue onset in educational environments.

Yawn Kinematics Mouth Aspect Ratio Facial Landmarks Fatigue Vectors MediaPipe
BA

B Anand

CV Engineer • Eye Closure & EAR Lead

Engineered the Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) drowsiness detection engine. Analyzed upper and lower eyelid coordinates to compute blink duration, eye closure velocities, and microsleep occurrences, establishing the core alertness metric utilized throughout the CAMS telemetry suite.

Eye Aspect Ratio Eyelid Kinematics Microsleep Analysis Web Telemetry Real-Time CV
Foundational Research Tribute

The v3.0 Architectural Studio of CAMS directly builds upon the core research breakthroughs established by Adarsh, Archa, and Anand. Their commitment to GPU-free, lightweight computer vision ensures that intelligent classroom monitoring remains accessible on standard edge educational hardware without costly cloud infrastructure.
