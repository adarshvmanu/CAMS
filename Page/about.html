System Architecture & V2.0 Evolution

A comprehensive technical overview of the Classroom Attention Monitoring System (CAMS) — from its foundational computer vision research to the V2.0 high-performance engineering overhaul.

V1.0 Foundations

Core Modules & Original Concepts

CAMS was conceptualized as a non-invasive, privacy-conscious classroom telemetry platform. By eliminating the requirement for expensive GPU clusters, the system uses Google MediaPipe FaceMesh to extract 468 facial landmark coordinates in real time on standard educational hardware.

1. Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR)

Tracks upper and lower eyelid coordinates to quantify blink frequency and eye closure intervals. Identifies microsleep episodes and prolonged drowsiness.

2. Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR)

Monitors the vertical opening and horizontal width of lips to measure yawning frequency, speech kinematics, and physical fatigue.

3. 3D Head Pose Estimation

Applies Perspective-n-Point (solvePnP) to estimate head orientation across Pitch, Yaw, and Roll axes, determining whether students face the instructor.

V2.0 Overhaul

Engineering Remediation, Math Fixes & Studio Architecture

The V2.0 upgrade addressed several mathematical, architectural, and performance bottlenecks identified during forensic system audits:

📐 Geometric Calibration & 3D Model

Fixed the transposed optical center in the camera matrix (cx = img_w/2, cy = img_h/2) and deployed a canonical 6-point anthropometric 3D face mesh ([1, 199, 33, 263, 61, 291]) aligned to OpenCV coordinates.

👄 Standardized MAR & Zero-Division Guard

Replaced inverted width/height math with standard MAR = height / width (0.04 - 0.50) guarded by max(width, 1e-5) and 15-frame yawn hysteresis.

👁️ Adaptive EAR & Eye-Shape Baseline

Recalibrated the sleep threshold from 0.22 to 0.16 and added a dynamic 75th-percentile open-eye baseline tracker, eliminating false "Sleeping" alerts for deep-set or almond eyes.

🎯 One-Click Zero-Pose Calibration

Compensates for laptop webcam screen tilt (MacBook $-16^\circ$ angle) via header calibration button, ensuring normal forward gaze is centered at 0°/0° without false penalties.

👥 Adaptive Multi-Student Tracker

Removed the hardcoded 4-seat ceiling. Uses dynamic Euclidean centroid matching with ID persistence, scaling smoothly from 1 student to 16+ students with responsive grid rendering.

⚡ In-Memory 30 FPS Zero-Disk Stream

Eliminated disk file I/O polling. The unified server.py streams video directly from RAM via threading conditions, delivering true 30 FPS video inside the web dashboard.

🚀 20× Faster Math & Zero-Copy Buffers

Replaced SciPy's heavy Euclidean distance with single-instruction C math.hypot and marked MediaPipe frames image.flags.writeable = False for direct zero-copy C++ processing.

📊 Executive Studio UI & CSV Export

Single-screen Obsidian Studio with glassmorphic cards, twin diagnostic meters (EAR/MAR), 100-point sliding window Plotly timeline, and instant CSV session export.

Future Scope

CAMS V2.0 Next-Generation Research Roadmap

Ongoing and future research milestones planned for subsequent CAMS iterations:

1. True Eye-in-Head Iris Gaze Tracking

Enabling MediaPipe Iris landmarks (468–477) to track pupil deflection vectors relative to eye corners ([33, 133] and [362, 263]). Detects when a student keeps their head upright but looks down at a phone or laptop.

2. Wide-Room Lecture Hall Scaling (30+ Students)

Deploying a two-stage "Detect-Then-Crop" pipeline pairing a dedicated face detector (YOLOv8n-Face) with ByteTrack Kalman filtering, passing cropped $192\times192$ facial patches to FaceMesh in round-robin batches.

3. Edge Privacy & RAM-Only Processing

Processing frames exclusively in volatile RAM with zero persistent video recording, ensuring strict compliance with GDPR, FERPA, and DPDP educational privacy frameworks.

4. Pedagogical Interaction Modes

Adding interactive toggles for Group Discussion and Lab Work modes, temporarily relaxing the forward-facing angle requirement when collaborative learning is active.
